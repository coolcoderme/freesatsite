About

A free desk for standardized-test practice.

FreeSAT is a small Flask app for students and teachers who want original SAT, PSAT, and ACT practice they can copy, print, download, or take in a Bluebook-like room. It ships with 10 labeled demo questions and a larger original bank so filters work without an API key. A key is used only if you want additional AI-written questions.

College Board, Bluebook, SAT, PSAT, and ACT are trademarks of their owners. This site is not affiliated with them and does not reproduce official exam items. AI-generated questions are extra practice, not official forms.
